# Alex's Caves — Battle Dinosaurs and Mutants Deep Underground

Alex's Caves 9minecraft Mod fills the underground world with massive custom biomes, unique monsters, and rare treasures. You find prehistoric zones with dinosaurs, radioactive tunnels with green mutants, and deep trenches full of glowing sea life. Each underground space gives you fresh armor, weapons, tools, and decorative blocks to collect. This mod turns basic mining trips into big adventures under the surface.

Adds massive underground biomes with custom blocks, plants, and atmospheric lighting.

## What this build is

An unofficial rebuild of **Alexthe668**'s original work, kept running on current Minecraft releases by 9Minecraft Studios. Not affiliated with the original author and not endorsed by them.

Most of the porting work sits in the mixin layer. Each Minecraft release moves or renames the methods this mod hooks into, so the hooks are re-pointed and re-tested against that version rather than rewritten. The behaviour underneath is upstream's and is deliberately left alone — that is the part that makes the mod feel right, and it is not ours to redesign.
